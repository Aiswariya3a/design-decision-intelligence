# Design Decision Intelligence — FORVIA HELLA presentation (Canva style)

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge (double-click works, no internet needed).
Style follows the Canva reference `AI Headlamp Workflow.pptx`: Montserrat Bold headings, IBM Plex Sans body,
navy #102A43, one blue accent #1570EF, pale-blue badges, white title/closing slides and #F6F8FA content slides.

## Presenting

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| → / Space / Enter / click | Next step or slide |
| ← / Backspace / right-click | Back |
| F | Fullscreen |
| N | Speaker notes (visible on screen — use on your own display only) |
| V | 60 s concept demo video (Esc closes) |
| B or . | Blackout |
| H | Key help |

The address bar shows the position (`#7.2` = slide 7, step 2).

## The 17 slides

1. Title, with the only disclaimer
2. What would make design work less tiring: the two needs we heard
3. The model shows how, not why
4. What the market offers today: three groups, and the gap our proposal fills
5. The example: a made-up mounting plate (hole 5.5 mm, allowed 5.9 to 6.1 mm)
6. The designer describes what the part must do
7. Similar past models, and the reason behind them
8. The design is checked as it changes (Fail / Review / Pass / Linked)
9. Options from past designs, with a clear recommendation (A / B / C, B recommended)
10. The engineer approves, and CATIA updates a copy
11. The assembly is checked before review (tool access 8.6 mm, no clash)
12. The design is checked against its simulation
13. How it is built
14. Every requirement in one place (checked by the first version / linked to analysis and test results)
15. Stages: Discovery, Proof of concept, Pilot with engineers, Wider rollout
16. Built to be trusted
17. Next step: Stage 0 discovery

A 7-badge story tracker runs along the bottom of slides 6 to 12.

## Disclaimer

One disclaimer sits at the bottom of the title slide: the plate, its limits and the past cases are made up for demonstration, and the headlamp images are illustrative. Individual slides carry no further labels. Confirm the Canva images' licence before external use.
